FluentSteps Privacy Policy

How FluentSteps collects, uses, shares, and protects information.

Effective August 17, 2026

1. Who we are

FluentSteps is operated by Yunus Ünver in Türkiye. In this policy, “FluentSteps,” “we,” and “us” refer to the operator of the FluentSteps mobile application and related services.

Privacy questions and requests can be sent to fluentsteps.support@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

Account and profile information

When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive information allowed by that provider, such as your name, email address, and provider user identifier. We also store onboarding choices such as English level, learning goal, and preferred practice time.

Learning and usage information

We store lesson progress, completed steps, camp selections, streaks, XP, memory and review items, practice attempts, aggregate learning events, and subscription access state. Learning analytics are designed to exclude raw audio, email addresses, names, prompts, and free-form text.

Speaking practice

The app accesses the microphone only after you start a speaking task. A short recording is sent through our backend to a speech-processing provider for transcription and feedback. Transcripts and speaking results may be stored with your learning attempts so the app can show progress and bring back useful practice. Raw recordings are not intended to be kept as a permanent cloud account record, although a temporary local copy may remain on the device while a task is active.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple or Google Play processes payments. We and RevenueCat receive transaction and entitlement information needed to unlock Pro access, restore purchases, and determine whether a subscription is active. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Technical and diagnostic information

We may receive app version, operating system, device type, timestamps, error details, and similar technical information needed to keep the service reliable. Production error reporting is configured not to send default personal information, screenshots, view hierarchies, session replay, or performance traces.

3. How we use information

  • Provide authentication, lessons, reviews, progress, and support.
  • Transcribe speaking practice and generate learning feedback.
  • Personalize review timing and identify weak or forgotten items.
  • Manage subscriptions, purchases, and restored access.
  • Protect accounts, prevent abuse, and diagnose service failures.
  • Understand aggregate learning outcomes and improve the product.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.

4. Service providers and sharing

We use service providers only as needed to operate FluentSteps:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, and backend functions.
  • Groq for speech transcription and speaking feedback processing.
  • RevenueCat for subscription entitlement management.
  • Sentry for privacy-restricted production error reporting.
  • Apple and Google for sign-in and platform services; Apple processes iOS purchases and Google Play processes Android purchases.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer with appropriate safeguards. We do not sell personal information and do not use it for cross-app advertising or tracking.

5. Storage, retention, and international processing

Information may be processed in countries where our service providers operate. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including access controls and encrypted network connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Account and learning information is generally kept while your account is active. When you delete your account, FluentSteps removes the account and associated progress from its active systems, subject to limited retention required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or routine backup expiration.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can decline microphone permission; non-speaking features remain available.
  • You can manage notification permission in device settings.
  • You can manage or cancel an iOS subscription in your Apple account, or an Android subscription in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
  • You can permanently delete your FluentSteps account from Settings.
  • You may ask to access, correct, delete, or receive information, or object to certain processing where applicable law provides these rights.

Send a request to fluentsteps.support@gmail.com. We may need to verify that the request relates to your account.

7. Children

FluentSteps is a general language-learning service and is not directed to children under 13. If local law requires parental consent at a higher age, users below that age should use the service only with the involvement and consent of a parent or guardian. Contact us if you believe a child provided personal information without required consent.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as FluentSteps changes. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when required by law. Continued use after an update is subject to the revised policy.
